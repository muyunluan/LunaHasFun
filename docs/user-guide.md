# End-User Guide

## Welcome Screen
When you open the app, you will see three large, colorful buttons:
1.  **Numbers (Math)**
2.  **Coins (Money)**
3.  **Calendar (Days)**

## Playing a Math Game
1. Tap the "Numbers" button.
2. You will see a math problem like `45 + 23 = ?`.
3. Use the large number buttons on the screen to type your answer.
4. Tap the "Check" button. If you are right, you get a star! If not, you get to try again.

## Counting Coins
1. Tap the "Coins" button.
2. Look at the pictures of the coins on the screen.
3. Add them up in your head (e.g., two dimes and a penny = 21 cents).
4. Type the total and check your answer.

## Calendar Questions
1. Tap the "Calendar" button.
2. The app will tell you what today is. It might ask: "What day is it in exactly one week?"
3. Pick the right answer from the choices given!
